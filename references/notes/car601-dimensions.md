# Raml heritage car 601: dimension sheet

*Compiled 2026-10-17. Photogrammetry: `photogrammetry/car601.py` (picks and scale) and `photogrammetry/underlay_601.py` (underlay). Photos: `../SOURCES.md` and `../manifests/car601.csv`. History and liveries: `heritage-dossier.md` §1 and `research-raw.md` §1.*

Confidence key: **S** sourced · **S?** generic/analog · **V** measured from photos · **E** estimate.

## Sources used

- **Main: transphoto 1920902** (M. Lazarov, El Raml terminal, 28 Feb 2024). A long-lens broadside of the +z side.
  - The window pitch is 77.7 px at both ends of the car, so perspective along the side is negligible and a linear scale works.
- **Head-on:** transphoto 2110423 (2025) and 1915990 (2024) give the front layout and the width/height ratios.
  - transphoto 2168484 shows the apron, steps and rails.
  - The 2012 Commons front shows the light-blue livery and board.
- **Interior:** transphoto 1702830 (Dec 2022), the saloon looking towards one end.
- **Liveries:** 2012 Commons (light blue), 2007 Parris (light blue), UTM 2023 and transphoto 2022–25 (red), TramTravels 2002 (yellow).

## Scale: no sourced dimension exists for 601

Three soft anchors agree within about 5 %. The adopted scale is **11.25 mm/px ± 7 %**.

| Anchor | Reading | mm/px |
|---|---|---|
| Pantograph-tower deck ≈ 4.5 m above rail. The Raml wire was raised for the 4.8 m double-deckers; the Kinki SD tower deck is 4.5 ± 0.2 m (see the [alexandria-tram-raml-221-223](https://github.com/imeshera/alexandria-tram-raml-221-223) notes) | deck at 397 px above rail | 11.3 |
| Wheel diameter, typical 1920s/30s bogie trams 800–860 mm | rim radius ≈ 37 px behind both inner axleboxes | 10.8–11.6 |
| Head-on width/dash ratio (2.68–2.72) with the broadside dash height, so width ≈ 196–217 px → 2.2–2.45 m. This is consistent with the Raml clearance (Kinki 2.42 m; the new cars are "wider", 2.65 m) | — | consistent |

**The gauge could not be used.** The rails are hidden or strongly oblique under the car in every head-on photo, and the focal length is unknown.

## Not resolvable / open

- **Absolute scale (±7 %).** A single sourced length or height would fix it.
  - Best bets: the APTA museum, or Richter, *Tramways Monthly* 2011.
  - Failing that, a head-on photo on straight track with the rails visible, plus the camera focal length.
- **Builder:** Oerlikon 1925 (UTM, en.wiki) or "Belgian, 1931" (press, and the car's own apron). Unresolved; see the dossier.
- **Front window layout differs between the photos.**
  - 2012, end with the side to the left: medium + big + corner windows.
  - 2025: medium + big + medium.
  - The model uses the symmetric layout (corner · intermediate · big centre · intermediate · corner) at both ends.
- **Centre side door.** The 2002 yellow car shows a double door near mid-length on one side; the 2024 broadside side has none. It was probably panelled over when the car became a museum car (2015). Not modelled.
- **Interior seat count, and whether the platforms keep the driver's stool.** E.
