# Raml heritage car 601: raw research notes

Compiled 2026-10-17. Raw research for the car 601 model; the photogrammetry and dimension sheet are in `car601-dimensions.md`, the summary in `heritage-dossier.md`.
Confidence key: **S** = stated by a cited source. **S?** = sourced, but sources conflict or the source is weak (fan database, generic type data). **V** = visual reading of a dated, cited photo. **E** = engineering estimate, to be checked by photogrammetry.
Source tags like [H1] are listed at the end; the numbering is shared with the sibling model repositories, so gaps are expected.

---

## 0. Headline findings

1. **Car 601 (the heritage Raml car) is the only survivor of the old Raml bogie cars.** Sources disagree on its builder and year: Oerlikon 1925 per UTM and en.wiki, versus "Belgian, 1931" per Egyptian press and the car's own apron. Details are in section 1. It was light blue and cream in 2012, and red and cream after the restoration relaunched on **6 Oct 2020**.
2. **Light blue/white vs yellow on single (non-coupled) cars.** The old single cars are the GT6s.
   - **Yellow (city):** ochre and cream (1970s); yellow, cream and blue stripe (c. 2000s–2010s).
   - **Green and white:** c. 2016 onward, city.
   - **Red and white, and blue and white:** a refurbished GT6 batch for **Raml lines 5/6** (Omar Lotfy street, El-Qa'ed Ibrahim terminus), c. 2017–2019 [H14].
   - The other light-blue single car on the Raml was **601** before 2020.
